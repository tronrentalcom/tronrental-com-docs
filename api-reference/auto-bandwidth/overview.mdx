## Auto Bandwidth

An Auto Bandwidth subscription keeps a fixed amount of bandwidth delegated to your TRON address, so that the address's transfers do not burn TRX for bandwidth. When a transfer uses up the delegated bandwidth, we renew it right away. There is no end date: the subscription runs until you pause or delete it.

### How it works

1. Create a subscription for an address with the `volume` of bandwidth to keep there — for one USDT transfer that is about 350.
2. We delegate `volume` bandwidth to the address and charge the fee for the first 24 hours.
3. We check the address **once a minute**, and **right after every outgoing USDT transfer** from it. Transfers of TRX, USDD or other tokens are picked up by the minute check.
4. When a transfer has used up the bandwidth we delegated, we charge one transfer fee and delegate `volume` again.

Renewing takes about two blocks (around 6 seconds). A second transfer sent before the renewal lands is paid from the address's free bandwidth, and burns TRX when that is not enough.

Creating a subscription does not charge anything in the request itself: we check that your balance covers the first day and one transfer, and the first delegation happens at the next check, usually within seconds.

### Cost

| Fee | When it is charged | Response fields |
|---|---|---|
| Subscription fee (per 24 hours) | In advance, when bandwidth is delegated, and every 24 hours after that while it stays on the address | `daily_trx` |
| Transfer fee | Once for each delegation a transfer has used up | `transfer_trx` = `transfer_bw_trx` + `transfer_fee_trx` |

Both fees equal the price of a one-hour bandwidth order of `volume` for your account: the bandwidth at your rate plus the fixed fee. `transfer_bw_trx` and `transfer_fee_trx` show that split. A subscription created with an API key is priced like a purchase made with that key; one created on the website, like a website purchase. To see the price before you subscribe, use [Auto Bandwidth Quote](/api-reference/auto-bandwidth/quote).

- A transfer paid entirely from the address's free bandwidth or its own stake does not touch our bandwidth, and there is no transfer fee for it.
- No subscription fee is charged while the subscription is paused, stopped or on auto-pause.
- Raising `volume` within a paid day charges the difference for the rest of that day. Lowering it is not refunded.
- If the address does not exist on-chain yet, the first delegation also activates it, and the activation is charged on top.

### Balance

Before every delegation your balance must cover one transfer fee, plus the subscription fee if the current day is not paid yet. When it does not, the subscription stops with `status: "stopped_balance"` and we take the bandwidth back. It resumes on its own after a deposit that covers a day and a transfer. If your Telegram account is linked, you get a message when it stops.

Creating or switching a subscription back on checks the same amount up front and returns `402 INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE` when the balance is short, with the amounts in `error.required_trx` and `error.available_trx`.

### Status

| `status` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `active` | We keep bandwidth on the address and renew it after each transfer |
| `free_bw` | Auto-pause: the address has at least `threshold` free bandwidth, so we hold nothing and charge nothing. We delegate again when the free bandwidth falls below `threshold` |
| `paused` | Switched off — by you, by a transfer (see `stop_after_transfer`), or by support. Nothing is held or charged |
| `stopped_balance` | Your balance did not cover the next delegation. Resumes on its own after a deposit |

| `stopped_reason` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `insufficient_balance` | Stopped for balance |
| `transfer` | Paused by a transfer of the subscription's token, because `stop_after_transfer` was on |
| `admin` | Switched off by support; you cannot switch it back on yourself — contact us |
| `null` | No stop on record, or paused by you |

`delivery_state` shows how the last delegation went: `ok`, `waiting_stake` (bandwidth is temporarily not available to delegate — we retry, nothing is charged) or `failed` (the delegation did not go through — we retry).

### Options

**Token.** `token` tells which token the address sends: `USDT`, `TRX`, `USDD`, `CUSTOM` (a TRC-20 token of your own, set in `contract`) or `OWN` (your own `volume`, optionally with a `contract`). It does not limit what we cover — any transfer that uses up our bandwidth is renewed. It sets the token for `stop_after_transfer`, and in the dashboard it suggests a `volume`: 350 for USDT and USDD, 300 for TRX. For a token of your own, [add its contract](/api-reference/auto-bandwidth/add-contract) first; the response tells how much bandwidth one transfer of it takes.

**Auto-pause.** With `auto_pause: true` we hold no bandwidth while the address's free daily bandwidth (600 per account) is at least `threshold`, and the subscription shows `free_bw`. `threshold` must then be between the larger of `volume` and 345, and 600.

**Free bandwidth notification.** With `notify_free_bw: true` we send a message to your Telegram and email each time the address's free bandwidth recovers to `threshold` after falling below it. It uses the same `threshold` and the same range, except that without auto-pause a `volume` above 600 allows `threshold: 600`.

**Stop after the next transfer.** With `stop_after_transfer: true` the first transfer of the subscription's token sent after you set it pauses the subscription with `stopped_reason: "transfer"`. That transfer is still covered.

**Changing the address.** Setting a new `address` in [Update Subscription](/api-reference/auto-bandwidth/update-subscription) moves the subscription: we take the bandwidth back from the old address, then delegate to the new one. Until the move is done, `address_change_to` shows the new address.

### Limits

- **One subscription per address** on an account. A second one for the same address returns `409 AUTO_BW_ADDRESS_TAKEN`.
- **20 subscriptions** per account; deleted ones do not count. Over the limit: `409 AUTO_BW_LIMIT_REACHED`.
- **20 token contracts** in your list. Over the limit: `409 AUTO_BW_CONTRACT_LIMIT`.
- **No contract addresses**, such as the USDT token contract: TRON does not allow delegating resources to them.
- **No addresses already covered by Smart Mode bandwidth or a transfer package with bandwidth**: `409 AUTO_BW_ADDRESS_COVERED`.
- **Request limits** on creating and editing subscriptions and on adding contracts — see [Rate Limits](/resources/rate-limits).

If Auto Bandwidth is temporarily switched off, creating a subscription, switching one on and adding a contract return `503 AUTO_BW_DISABLED`. Nothing is created or changed — try again later.

### Compared with Auto Refill and Smart Mode

| | Auto Bandwidth | [Bandwidth Auto Refill](/api-reference/auto-refill/overview) | [Smart Mode](/api-reference/smart-mode/overview) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resource | Bandwidth | Bandwidth (or energy) | Energy, plus optional bandwidth |
| When it delivers | Kept on the address, renewed after each transfer that uses it | When the level drops below your threshold | On every outgoing USDT transfer |
| What you pay | A fee per 24 hours plus a fee per used delegation | Each refill, as a regular order | A daily subscription plus each USDT transfer |
