## Get Order

Returns the current status and full details of an energy or bandwidth order.

### Path parameters

<ParamField path="order_id" type="number" required>
  Order ID — the `id` returned by Buy Energy or Buy Bandwidth.
</ParamField>

### Response

Returns the full order object including `status`, `type`, `volume`, `price_trx`, `delegate_txid`, and timestamps.

<ResponseField name="fixed_fee_trx" type="string">
  Fixed fee of this order, charged **once per order** regardless of volume. It is **already included** in `price_trx`. Reflects your own fee if a custom fee, discount or waiver applies to your account.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="activation_id" type="number | null">
  ID of the address activation this order paid for, or `null` when it paid for none.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="activation_cost_trx" type="string | null">
  What that activation cost, in TRX, **on top of** `price_trx` — this is the difference between the order price and the amount that left your balance.
</ResponseField>

<Note>
**`null`, not `"0"`.** Both fields are `null` when the order paid for no activation — either the recipient account already existed, or somebody else's transaction created it. Note the difference from `activation_cost_trx` on the Buy Energy response, which is the string `"0"` in that case: comparing against `"0"` here would never match.
</Note>

<ResponseField name="fail_reason" type="string | null">
  Why a `failed` order failed, as a code from the **Failure reasons** table on [Get Order](/api-reference/orders/get-order); `null` for any other status. Treat a code you do not know as `internal_error`: new codes may be added.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="refunded_trx" type="string | null">
  What went back to your balance, in TRX, as a decimal string: the full `price_trx` for `failed`, the price of the undelivered part for `partial`; `null` when nothing was refunded.
</ResponseField>

### Order statuses

| Status | Final | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| `created` | no | Order recorded, delivery not started yet |
| `processing` | no | A large energy order (over 1,000,000) is being delivered in parts |
| `submitted_unknown` | no | Delegation broadcast, waiting for confirmation in a block — bandwidth orders and energy orders under 32,000 |
| `filled` | yes | Energy or bandwidth delegated |
| `partial` | yes | A large energy order was delivered in part; the price of the undelivered part is back on your balance |
| `failed` | yes | Delivery failed; the full price is back on your balance |
| `reclaiming` | no | Delivered; the resource is being taken back — at the end of the rental or after [Reclaim Order](/api-reference/orders/reclaim-order) |
| `reclaimed` | yes | Delivered; the rental period is over, or you called [Reclaim Order](/api-reference/orders/reclaim-order), and the resource was taken back |
| `reclaim_failed` | yes | Delivered; taking the resource back after the rental did not go through on our side. Nothing for you to do |

An order in progress normally becomes final within seconds, at most minutes. Poll this endpoint every
5–10 seconds, or subscribe to the `order.updated` [webhook](/api-reference/webhooks/overview) to get the
final state pushed to you. If an order stays in progress for more than 15 minutes, contact support with
its ID.

A final status does not change, with one exception: a `filled` order may later move to `reclaimed` or
`reclaim_failed` when its rental ends. A `failed` or `partial` order is never delivered further or
charged again. If its purchase response had `provider_outcome: "unknown"`, the refunded energy may
still reach the address within minutes, and you are not charged for it — see
[Buy Energy](/api-reference/energy/buy-energy).

### Failure reasons

A `failed` order always returns its full price to your balance, and `refunded_trx` shows the amount. `fail_reason` says what went wrong and whether a retry makes sense:

| `fail_reason` | What happened | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `insufficient_supply` | Not enough resource was available at that moment | Retry later |
| `delivery_timeout` | Delivery was not confirmed in time, so we refunded. For energy, the resource may still reach the address within minutes — you are not charged for it | Check the address before you retry |
| `order_rejected` | The order was declined for this address — for example, when the address already has its own staked resource | The same order may fail again; retrying later or with another address may help |
| `transaction_failed` | The network rejected the delegation transaction | Retry |
| `address_not_activated` | The recipient address does not exist on-chain yet | Activate it with [Activate Address](/api-reference/energy/activate-address) and retry |
| `internal_error` | Any other failure on our side | Retry; if it repeats, contact support with the order ID |

```json
{
  "id": 12346,
  "status": "failed",
  "price_trx": "2.15",
  "fail_reason": "insufficient_supply",
  "refunded_trx": "2.15"
}
```

<Note>
New codes may be added to this list. Treat a code you do not know as `internal_error`.
</Note>
