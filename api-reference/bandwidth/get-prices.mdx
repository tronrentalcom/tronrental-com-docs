## Get Bandwidth Prices

Returns the public bandwidth rates for `1h` and `1d`, the order volume limits, the fixed fee and the
parameters of the discount on minute terms (`5m` … `59m`).

**This endpoint is public — no authentication required.** It always returns the public rates. For
the exact amount of an order at your own rate, use [Quote Bandwidth](/api-reference/bandwidth/quote).

### Response

<ResponseField name="price_per_day_trx" type="string">
  TRX for 1,000 bandwidth units for 24 hours at the hourly rate: `price_sun_1h × 24 / 1000`. A `1d`
  order is priced by `price_sun_1d` instead.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="price_sun_1h" type="number">
  Public rate for `1h`, SUN per 1 bandwidth unit. Minute terms are priced from it.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="price_sun_1d" type="number">
  Public rate for `1d`, SUN per 1 bandwidth unit
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="min_volume" type="number">
  Smallest order, in bandwidth units (1)
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="max_volume" type="number">
  Largest order, in bandwidth units (100,000)
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="fixed_fee_trx" type="string">
  Standard fixed fee, charged once per order for any duration. Your own fee may differ —
  [Quote Bandwidth](/api-reference/bandwidth/quote) returns it.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="short_min_minutes" type="number">
  Shortest minute term (5, i.e. `"5m"`)
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="short_max_minutes" type="number">
  Longest minute term (59, i.e. `"59m"`). An hour is `"1h"`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="short_max_discount_pct" type="string">
  Discount off the hourly rate on the shortest term, in percent. It falls linearly to zero on the
  longest term: `discount % = short_max_discount_pct × (59 − minutes) / 54`. It applies to the rental
  only, not to the fixed fee. `"0"` means minute terms cost the hourly rate.
</ResponseField>
